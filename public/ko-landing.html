Before you start

Questions

Do I need to know Korean before starting?
+

Not at all. Lv.0 Seed starts with reading Hangul from scratch. If you can't read Korean yet, that's exactly where we start — and you'll be reading by the end of Week 2.

How long does each daily session take?
+

About 15 minutes. You can do it on the subway, during lunch, before bed. The 7 steps are designed to fit real life — not require it to be rearranged around them.

Are the sentences from real K-dramas or K-pop songs?
+

No — and that's intentional. All 56 sentences are original, written specifically for this curriculum. They capture the K-emotional tone and rhythm while avoiding any copyright issues. You learn to feel the language, not memorize specific lines.

What makes this different from Duolingo or Pimsleur?
+

Focus. We do one sentence a day, deeply. You learn to feel Korean — its rhythm, its emotion, its cultural weight — not just match words to pictures or repeat audio drills. Breadth is fast. Depth is what stays.

Can I start as a complete beginner?
+

Yes. Take the level test and we'll place you at Lv.0 Seed if needed. You'll learn to read Hangul in the first two weeks — it's a phonetic alphabet and most people crack it in a few days.
